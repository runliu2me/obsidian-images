Wool spinning room, Kammgarnspinnerei Pfaffendorf, Leipzig, about 1925.
Machines stand in one long row. Shafts and belts run overhead and drop onto the machines.
Photograph: Atelier Hermann Walter. License: Public domain.
This is a German spinning mill about 1925, not an 1899 American steam-powered plant. No steam engine is in the frame.
