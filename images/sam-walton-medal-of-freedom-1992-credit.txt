President George H. W. Bush and Sam Walton, 17 March 1992, Bentonville, Arkansas. Bush presents the Presidential Medal of Freedom.
Walton is the man on the viewer's right: white Walmart cap, dark suit, seated in a wheelchair. Bush is on the left, in a gray suit and a blue Walmart cap.
Photo credit: George Bush Presidential Library and Museum. License: Public domain.
This is a 1992 ceremony. It is not the meeting where Walton talked to Procter & Gamble about diapers.
