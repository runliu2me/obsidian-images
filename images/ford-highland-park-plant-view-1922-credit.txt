View of the Ford Highland Park plant, published in Farm Mechanics, 1 February 1922. Bernard L. Johnson.
The picture itself is captioned "90 Acres Under Roof", "Ford Motor Company, Highland Park, Michigan", and "Capacity, 1,000,000 Cars Annually". That capacity line is the printed claim on the picture, not a separate production audit.
