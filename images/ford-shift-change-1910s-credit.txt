Shift change outside the Ford Motor Company plant. Detroit Publishing Company. Commons dates it between 1910 and 1920 and describes it as the 4 o'clock shift at the Ford assembly plant in Detroit.
Glass walls and the roof sign are visible. People are walking out. This is not the January 1914 crowd of job applicants, and no fire hose is in the frame.
Highland Park is a separate city beside Detroit. The Commons caption says Detroit.
