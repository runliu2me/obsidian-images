Henry Ford between the ten millionth Model T and a Ford Quadricycle. Detroit Publishing Co., 4 June 1924.
The Model T is lettered for a New York to San Francisco Lincoln Highway trip. The small vehicle is the 1896 Quadricycle, not a 1908 or 1925 Model T.
