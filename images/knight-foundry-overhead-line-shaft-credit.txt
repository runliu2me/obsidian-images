Overhead line shaft and belts, Knight Foundry machine shop, Sutter Creek, California
Photographer: Jet Lowe, 1996, for the Historic American Engineering Record.
HAER CAL,3-SUCRK,1-45. Library of Congress digital ID hhh.ca2269.
The record says most machines are belt-driven and power comes from the Knight turbine on the far wall. This is not an 1899 steam-powered plant, and the steam engine is not in the frame.
