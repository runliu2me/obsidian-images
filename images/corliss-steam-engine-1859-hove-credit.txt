An 1859 Corliss horizontal steam engine, preserved at the British Engineerium, Hove. Photographed 10 October 2010. People standing beside the flywheel show the size.
Commons names the builder in the description and marks the photograph public domain. Photographer recorded as The Voice of Hassocks.
This is a museum engine in England, photographed in 2010, with visitors and a cafe around it. It is not the left side of an 1899 American factory drawing, and no line shaft is attached in the frame.
