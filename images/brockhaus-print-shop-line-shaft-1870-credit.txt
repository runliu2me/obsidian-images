Engraving of the Brockhaus printing-house machine hall, 1870. Eduard and Milly Brockhaus visiting the room.
Overhead shafts run the length of the hall and belts drop to the machines, which stand in rows.

This is a print shop in Leipzig, drawn in 1870, not a photograph of an 1899 American machine shop, and not a steam engine.
