Small electric motor with reversing and a foot control, used by watchmakers. Circa 1935. Maker recorded on Commons: P. E. Calame and Co.
Auckland War Memorial Museum, object AM 2004.51.30. Photograph license: CC BY 4.0. https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
Keep the Commons attribution: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Electric_motor_(AM_2004.51.30-4).jpg
This motor is far smaller than a factory unit drive, and it is about twenty years after the 1913 Ford line. It illustrates "motors kept getting smaller", not a Ford machine.
Unmodified.
