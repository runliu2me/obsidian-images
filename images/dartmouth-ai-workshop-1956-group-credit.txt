Dartmouth Summer Research Project on Artificial Intelligence, group on the lawn, 1956
Seven people. Not the 2006 AI@50 reunion, and not the whole summer meeting.
Photographer, by his own letter of 8 November 1956: Nathaniel Rochester. "Enclosed is a print of the photograph I took of the Artificial Intelligence group."
Courtesy stated on the source page: Nicholas E. Rochester.
Identifications used here follow Grace Solomonoff's reading of that letter, Paul More, and a 2023 confirmation from the office of Brenda Milner. Back row, left to right: Oliver Selfridge, Nathaniel Rochester, Marvin Minsky, John McCarthy. Front left: Ray Solomonoff. Front middle: Peter Milner, not Trenchard More. Front right: Claude Shannon.
The wider print shows the left side of Dartmouth Hall. IEEE Spectrum published a crop of the same photograph.
